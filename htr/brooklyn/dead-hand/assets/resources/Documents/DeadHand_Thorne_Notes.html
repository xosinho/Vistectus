E. THORNE — 74 DEGRAW

Observations. Keep in the drawer.

If found, get it to Maj. Payne. He'll know what it's worth.

14 JUN 21 — 3 wks in the house. Basement, rear wall, left corner as you

come down the stairs: cold. Not damp. No draft. Boiler 10 ft away, running.

Bought a thermometer. Old man's hobby. Fine.

20 OCT 21 — Corner reads 16° under the rest of the room. Same

every night. Laid a level on the slab: true. No crack, no seep, no pipe

under it per the old plans. Cold with nothing making it cold.

22 JAN 22 — Frost on the slab. Heated basement, 58° in the room.

An oval, maybe the size of a manhole cover. Held a candle over it.

The flame leaned in toward the middle and stayed leaning. Did it three times.

Breath fogs over the spot and nowhere else.

Not telling anybody this part.

12 MAR 22 — SALUTE.

  • S 1 white panel van, 2 pax. Never get out.
  • A Parked, engine off. Watching the house, not the street.
  • L Across Degraw, 3 doors down. Same spot each time.
  • U No markings. Commercial plates, mud on the numbers.
  • T 2210 to 0340. Third night this week.
  • E Tinted rear glass. Roof vent that isn't for air.

Same hours, every time. Trains always on time.

19 MAY 22 — Letter. Holding company, Italian name, wants to

buy the house. Cash. No inspection. Over asking. Said no.

2 JUN 22 — Second letter. More money. Said no.

Nobody pays over asking for a house they haven't looked at.

Unless they have. From the van.

NONNA'S STORY

Beatrice Tonello — Nonna's mother. Venice, 1919.

She saw a family in a great house call up the dead and make them speak.

She was a girl. She told it the rest of her life and nobody believed her.

Nonna used to say the Tonello blood remembers. I thought that was

the kind of thing old women say over coffee.

The cold started the month I moved in.

20 AUG 22 — Wrote to Payne. Kept it short. Didn't want to read

like a crazy old man on a porch. No answer.

8 OCT 22 — Cellar door, garden side. Fresh scratches on the lock.

Nothing taken. Nothing in this house worth taking.

They don't want anything in the house I own.

They want the floor.

11 NOV 22 — Van's gone. 3 nights now.

Not relieved. A unit pulls off a target when the job's about to change.

14 NOV 22 — Spot reads 33°. Frost all the way to the wall.

If something happens to me: it's the floor. Rear wall, left corner.

Don't let anybody dig it up.

Payne — the trains were never on time. Get here.

— E.
