I. G. — private. Not for the house.

26 October — before dawn

Vendramin five nights silent. G. says the harbour. The harbour says nothing. In the quarterly accounts tonight: cold storage, Red Hook, special handling, a sum that would keep a restaurant's meat for ten years. We ship nothing cold. I asked what it stores. He smiled and said, patience, Ilaria. That is three now. I have stopped believing in reassignments.

31 October

The Tonello line again. Third assay this year. The Rating holds at 9, as it did in May of last year, as it will next month if he asks again. He did not look at the number. He looked at me, to see whether I understood it. The carrier is living, a veteran on Degraw Street, and G. has had that house watched since the spring. A van. I have seen the invoices for it.

4 November

Wrote to the Seat. Named the three. Named the storage. Named the house. Sent it by a hand that does not pass through this building, and kept the copy here, where I sleep. If I am wrong I will be forgiven. If I am right, it will not matter much whether I am forgiven.

11 November

Workmen in the basement all week. His own people, the ones who carry for Pellegrino's. New rooms for guests of the family who may need a day's rest. Six doors. Five open for inspection. The sixth is locked, and I was not given the key. An auditor who is refused a door is being told something. I have written down which door.

42
17 November — near dawn

The carrier died yesterday at St. Jude's. A man whose asset has just died should be grieving the asset. G. is not grieving. The body went to Pellegrino's for what they are calling the premium treatment. I have audited that phrase before and never liked what it cost.

Tonight a lawyer from one of the Venetian names was asking about the dead man's heirs. He wants the man He never wanted the man. He wants the house.

20 November

Answer from the Seat, by a road I did not expect. Patience. We have asked friends outside the family to look. Outside the family. I asked the Elders for discretion and they have given Brooklyn to strangers. I do not know which strangers. I do not like that I will not be told.— whose friends?

Two nights ago, after the police had been through Pellegrino's, they brought something down to the sixth room. Wrapped. Carried the way one carries the dead.

43
24 November — Thanksgiving, the mortals call it

Pellegrino's is gone. The radio says gas. It was not gas. Whatever they carried down on the eighteenth was taken out of it in time. Tonight, a woman, brought down the same stairs. I heard her until I did not.

G. has been very courteous to me this week. Men grow courteous to those they have already decided about.

If this book is found, the sixth door is the one at the end, on the left, past the tunnel stair.
