Copia — trattenuta

Brooklyn, the fourth of November, 2022

To the Seat of the Hecate, Venice
for the attention of the Elders, and of no one in Brooklyn

Your Excellencies,

I write under the obligation of my office, which requires me to report what I cannot reconcile. I have tried for two months to reconcile what follows. I cannot.

Three members of our faction attached to the Brooklyn house have ceased to report. Each disappearance was explained to me by the Director, courteously and without paper:

  • Ottavio Sanudo, who carried this house's dispatches to the Seat. Last seen the second of September. The Director tells me he was recalled. If he reached you, Your Excellencies will know this letter to be the anxiety of an old auditor, and may burn it.
  • Livia Moro, assayer, keeper of the duplicate Purity register. Absent from our audit of the thirtieth of September. Reassigned, I am told, to the Director's private business. The duplicate register has not been seen since.
  • Teodoro Vendramin, observer to the Family's shipping office at Red Hook. Silent since the twenty-first of October. On the Director's business at the harbour, I am told. The harbour office keeps no record of him.

I note that each of the three answered to this Seat before they answered to the Director. I note that I am the last of that description in Brooklyn.

I note also the Director's work. He has instructed me to re-test samples from a single Venetian line more often than any audit requires, and receives the results as a man receives news of an investment rather than of a bloodline. He takes an interest in a private house in Carroll Gardens, a house belonging to no one of ours, which he has had watched for many months. And the Family's accounts carry a cost for refrigerated storage at Red Hook, under special handling, which I am not permitted to examine.

I do not accuse the Director. I have no proof to offer Your Excellencies, only a pattern, and a pattern is what an auditor is for. I ask that the Seat make its inquiries through channels the Director does not control, and that no answer come to me through this house.

I remain at my post, as my office requires. Should I also fall silent, Your Excellencies will understand what the silence means.

With the obedience of my office,

Ilaria Grimani

Auditor of the Purity · Brooklyn
