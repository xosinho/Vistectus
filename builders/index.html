The World Builder

Where worlds are made, and where Storytellers and Dungeon Masters keep their chronicles and campaigns up to date.

Who the Builder is for

Invited builders make a new world here, step by step: its system, its name and pitch, its factions, the options players may choose from, its first chronicle or campaign, the NPCs and resources to begin with. The site’s admin approves it, and then it appears on the site.

Storytellers and Dungeon Masters use the Builder’s workshop to keep adding to their chronicles as play goes on: NPCs, adventurers’ cards, handouts, maps, reference links and places, each hidden from the players until you choose to show it.

Players do not need the Builder: they sign in on their chronicle’s own pages.

Sign in

The Builder is not connected yet, so signing in is switched off for now.

We email you a one-time sign-in link. No password to remember. The first link also creates your account.

Signing in works for invited builders, Storytellers, Dungeon Masters and the site’s admins. Have a world you want to bring here? Host a World, and the site’s admin can invite you as a builder.
