About

VisTectus is where tabletop worlds are built, run and played — one home for a setting and every campaign set inside it.

For builders

Build

Publish a world at any stage — a single map, one city, a full setting — and keep expanding it as the ideas arrive.

For game masters

Run

Organise every campaign as its own chronicle, so several tables can share one world without colliding.

For players

Play

Give the table a single, always-current reference: character sheets, backstories, the people they have met and the rules they play by.

One world. Every campaign. A single source of truth for the table.
