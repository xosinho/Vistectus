About

Vistectus exists to give worldbuilders a place to publish what they have created.

A setting rarely arrives complete. Vistectus is built to receive a world at its origin, in whatever form it has so far taken, and to provide the structure for its expansion: a durable record of what has been established, and room for everything that follows.

Within a world, each campaign is kept as a separate chronicle, so that several tables may run in the same setting without interference. Every chronicle serves as the single repository for its players — character histories, the figures they have encountered, house rules, handouts and reference material — maintained between sessions.

The name joins the Latin vis, force, to the Greek tekton, builder.
