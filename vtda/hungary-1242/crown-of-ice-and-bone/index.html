The chronicle

Hungary, in the year of Our Lord 1242. The Mongol horde has burned the kingdom from the Carpathians to the Danube, and its king has fled to the coast. In the dark beneath the ashes, the Cainites who survived the winter count what is left, and what can now be taken.
