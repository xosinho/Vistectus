IMAGES REQUIRED BY index.html
=============================

index.html expects eleven files in this folder. Portrait art is displayed at
3:4; save it at roughly 900 x 1200 px. The hero is displayed full-bleed; save
it at roughly 1600 px on the long edge. JPEG, quality ~80, is ample.

    hero.jpg        Title card — the lord on the tower above the burning city
    ventrue.jpg     Ventrue      — The Patricians
    tremere.jpg     Tremere      — The Usurpers
    nosferatu.jpg   Nosferatu    — The Priors
    lasombra.jpg    Lasombra     — The Magisters
    toreador.jpg    Toreador     — The Aesthetes
    gangrel.jpg     Gangrel      — The Wolf's-Heads
    malkavian.jpg   Malkavian    — The Cassandras
    brujah.jpg      Brujah       — The Zealots
    ravnos.jpg      Ravnos       — The Charlatans
    giovanni.jpg    Giovanni     — The Merchant-Necromancers
